The ordering and kitchen system for restaurants

Butta is a ready-made system for food service. Customers order from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or your website, and the order lands on your kitchen screen.

What's included

Order intake

Orders from every channel in one place

Menu and platform sync

Menu changes appear everywhere at once

Customer messages

Order confirmations and status updates

Live dashboards

See open orders, kitchen load and reports

How it works

  1. Customer orders

    Customers open your digital menu from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or your website, choose and customise items, and send the order.

  2. Order is checked

    Butta checks the order and confirms it with the customer straight away. Nobody on your team retypes it.

  3. Kitchen sees it

    The order appears on the Kitchen Display System, sorted so your team knows what to cook first.

  4. Payment and POS

    Customers pay online or at pickup or delivery. If you have a POS, orders sync to it so nothing is entered twice.

  5. Customer updates

    Customers get WhatsApp updates as the order moves along, so your staff aren't fielding "is it ready?" calls.

The result: your team cooks and serves, and orders reach the kitchen on their own, at any hour.
